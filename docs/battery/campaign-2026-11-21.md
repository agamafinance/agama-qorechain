After the campaign:

- explorer check, one transaction per kind the batteries sent (faucet, encryption key, sealed application, deposit,
  instant and queued withdrawals, queue push, full exit, revocation): **9/9** "quantum-safe · verified on-chain",
  ML-DSA-87
- post-quantum audit: **584/584** transactions of Agama's accounts since the switch hybrid-verified; no EVM window
  open; an ECDSA-only transaction from the operator and from the relayer refused by the chain
- onboarding, rerun with the fixes below: **7/7** (985.14 agUSD deposited from the Earn card)

Both stops were the tests' own assumptions, not the protocol:

1. The battery expected 80% of its new deposits to leave for Ethereum. After round 1's exits had drained the 10%
   buffer, the keeper first refilled it and bridged only the excess (its log shows the allocation and the Sepolia
   relay at 17:44 UTC). The check now asks for what the keeper promises: money sent, both legs equal, buffer under
   12%. Fixed in `b0a6eb7`.
2. The onboarding test needs a QoreX account whose ML-DSA-87 key is registered, which QoreX does with an account's
   first transaction. The campaign gave it untouched indexes, so the app showed its "no post-quantum key on chain
   yet" notice, as designed. `keeper/src/new-lender.ts` now prepares the account the way QoreX would (`981a698`),
   and the deposit step waits for the new balance before typing (`49d608b`).

Cost: 108 QOR from the operator over the three rounds (lender funding, keeper decisions, ten-part data room epochs,
bridge legs). `keeper/src/health.ts` reads the runway after it.
