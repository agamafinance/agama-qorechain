# Findings

Everything the tests, the end-to-end runs, the batteries and the live keeper turned up, and what was done.
Latest battery: [docs/battery](battery/), **42/42** with 8 lenders against the production keeper.

| # | Found by | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundry invariant (seed 3) | `AgUSD._recognizeGain` underflowed (`vested - fee`) once a loss left the vault holding less than the queue is owed, so no upward mark could ever be recorded again. | **Fixed**: a gain is capped at what holders own; regression test. The Whitechain vault it was ported from has the same code (open there). |
| 2 | Live keeper | A NAV report read `totalSent` from a node one block behind the vault's `deployedAssets`: an allocation looked lost and the vault was marked down 5% (price 0.955). | **Fixed**: every chain read at one block; a mark-down only once it persists 15 min. The wrong mark was undone with the fee at 0 (`remark.ts`), so holders paid no fee on the recovery. |
| 3 | Gas receipts | QoreChain charges the whole gas limit, not the gas used: round ceilings cost 3 to 5 times the work. | **Fixed**: limits are the estimate plus 25%. |
| 4 | Redeploy e2e | Publishing a KEM key already in the registry is refused (`pqc` 253), which failed the whole QoreX transaction. | **Fixed**: the registry message is sent only when the key differs (keeper and app). |
| 5 | Gas tank | A spending-rule refusal lands with code 0; the refusal is an `abstractaccount_execution_failed` event, and the REST tx does not carry `msg_responses`. | **Fixed**: the event is read; e2e checks a payment over the limit is refused. |
| 6 | Browser e2e | The QoreX test double reads `account_info`, which the REST proxy did not forward: every signature failed with "account does not exist". | **Fixed**: route allowed. |
| 7 | Browser e2e | A fresh browser has no QoreSeal seed for an account whose key is already on chain. | **By design**: the app offers restore from the backup file; the e2e keeps its seed like a user keeps the backup. |
| 8 | CI | Three web components were never committed; the local build hid it. | **Fixed**. |
| 9 | CI | `next/font/google` fetches fonts at build time and failed at random on GitHub runners. | **Fixed**: Inter and Space Grotesk self-hosted. |
| 10 | Battery 1 | An instant withdrawal sent in the same block as large queued exits reverts: a queued claim settles from the buffer as soon as it is made (FIFO), so the buffer the app saw is gone. | **Mitigated**: correct by design (first in, first out); the app now says so and offers to queue, and agUSD is untouched. |
| 11 | Battery 1 | The issuer wires a subscription to the book one keeper tick after it arrives, not in the same block. | **Not a bug**: the battery waits for it. |
| 12 | Dependencies | `@qorechain/qore-cli` was refused by the team's npm guard (published 4 days before, no provenance). | **Decision**: left out; the same check is done with QoreSign `verifyHybridTx`. |
| 13 | Threat model / QoreScan | The Ethereum leg's attestation was secp256k1 only (Ethereum has no post-quantum precompile). | **Fixed**: hybrid attestation, secp256k1 + ML-DSA-44 (FIPS 204) verified in Solidity, or + Merkle WOTS+; no owner. See PQ_ETHEREUM.md. |
| 14 | Battery 2 | Checks read too tightly: per-second profit unlocking moves the price between a deposit and the next read, and a claim the buffer covers is paid and deleted inside the transaction that makes it. | **Not a bug**: the battery values claims before sending them and tolerates unlocking. |
| 15 | Battery 3 | A refused lender's failing transactions cost their whole gas limit (finding 3 again) and drained its QOR mid-battery; two batteries had also left 18 QOR idle in test accounts. | **Fixed**: refused profiles get what their failures cost, tight limits on transactions meant to fail, and `sweep.ts` returns leftovers to the operator. |
| 16 | Browser e2e, after the battery | Right after a transaction, the public REST node can still report the previous account sequence; QoreX then builds the next one with the old sequence and the chain refuses it (`account sequence mismatch`). A lender clicking twice quickly would hit it. | **Fixed**: the app retries once after 4 s (nothing is spent on a refused sequence); browser e2e 9/9. |
| 17 | Migration to the post-quantum Ethereum leg | Nonces were `keccak(chainid, domain, counter)`: the redeployed Ethereum messenger counted from 1 into nonces its predecessor had spent on QoreChain, which keys spent nonces by nonce alone. Its first return ($2,493.43) was burned on Ethereum and refused on QoreChain. | **Fixed**: the messenger's address is in the nonce, with a regression test that fails without it. Repaired live: dust burns past the spent range, everything brought home, custody re-sent the burned amount, the leg redeployed (v3). No lender lost anything; the e2e lender's queued exit was paid. |
| 18 | Battery 5, after the migration | The production keeper restored an older scan position and retried every historical QoreChain to Ethereum message against the new messenger, which refused them (addressed to its predecessor); a minute of retries each stalled the whole run, compliance included. | **Fixed**: only messages addressed to the live messenger are relayed. |
| 19 | Battery 6 | The data room sealed each epoch key to every approved lender in one envelope, 1.6 kB per reader; at 36 readers (about 60 kB) QoreChain's public RPC refused the request with HTTP 403 and no new epoch could start. | **Fixed**: DataRoom v2 distributes the key in parts of eight readers plus the auditor, the manager signing every part's hash at once; each lender reads only its part. Redeployed; 36 lenders in 5 parts. |
| 20 | Testing with the real QoreX 0.4.3 | QoreX now refuses to sign any message it cannot display, and it has no display for `MsgCrossVMCall`: every lender action (faucet, key, dossier, deposit) was refused in the real wallet, while the test double still signed it. QoreX 0.4 also keeps `MsgRegisterKEMKey` and EVM windows to itself. A first fix (an authz grant to a relayer, ) still asked every new lender to open an EVM window in QoreX's own screens and renew it every ~6 hours: the chain refuses a relayed cross-VM call without one (verified on the testnet). | **Fixed with PqAccounts** (`contracts/src/account`): each lender's Agama account is a contract controlled only by its owner's ML-DSA-87 key. The lender signs a plain-text message in QoreX (`signArbitrary`); the relayer verifies both halves, submits it, and the account checks the ML-DSA-87 signature on chain with `pqcVerify` against the key from `pqcKeyStatus` before calling Agama. No window, no grant, no gas for the lender. Nothing else was redeployed: the factory owns the Asset Kit guard and exempts only accounts it deploys for owners with a proven key. Foundry 10 tests; `keeper/src/pq-check.ts` 7/7 on the testnet (replay, forged call, wrong key refused, and the forged call refused by the contract itself). |
| 21 | QoreChain's explorer, after PqAccounts | The relayer's 69 calls to the PqAccountFactory, and every write of the keeper on QoreChain, were EVM transactions: ECDSA alone, admitted by EVM windows. The explorer marked them *not quantum-safe*, even though each PqAccount verified its owner's ML-DSA-87 signature on chain. Moving them to the native lane has a catch: a `MsgCrossVMCall` executes the contract but its EVM logs are not served by `eth_getLogs`, and the bridge (`MessageSent`), the data room (`EpochPart`, `ReportPublished`), the decision letters (`Approved`, `Rejected`), the officer's provenance check (`DossierFiled`, `Executed`) and the price history (`NavReported`) all read logs. The SDK's `closeEvmWindowMsg({ sender })` form fails ("decoding bech32 failed: invalid character ' '"): the function takes the address itself. | **Fixed**: the keeper and the relayer (keeper and app) send every QoreChain write as a hybrid-signed (secp256k1 + ML-DSA-87) native `MsgCrossVMCall`, simulated first, gas at 2.8 times the EVM estimate plus 400k, retried on `out of gas` and on `account sequence mismatch`, accepted only on code 0 and `crossvm_response executed`, with a descriptive memo. Readers rebuild from the committed transaction what logs carried, each fact checked against state (ARCHITECTURE.md): the bridge message is rebuilt byte for byte (`bridge-check.ts`: 37/37 identical to the logged ones; a native round trip crossed both ways), the officer re-verifies the owner's ML-DSA-87 signature off chain for the one nonce that verifies. Every role's window is closed; `pq-audit.ts` and the protocol e2e check that every transaction since the switch is hybrid-verified and that the chain refuses an ECDSA-only one from the operator and the relayer. The only EVM transaction left is a contract creation, deployment only. |
